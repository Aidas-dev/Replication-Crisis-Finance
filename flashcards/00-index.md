# Cue cards

One card per slide. Each card fits one phone screen.
Open the card that matches the slide on screen. Cards 02 to 14 carry the same
number as the footer, 1 / 14 to 13 / 14.

Plan: 12 minutes of talk, then questions. The slot allows 10 to 15 minutes.

## Timing

- 01 Title: 15 seconds
- 02 The claim under test: 45 seconds
- 03 The two challenges: 55 seconds
- 04 Three moves: 40 seconds
- 05 The model: 70 seconds
- 06 Why shrinkage wins: 60 seconds
- 07 The data: 50 seconds
- 08 The replication ladder: 70 seconds
- 09 Size groups and themes: 50 seconds
- 10 Out-of-sample decay: 60 seconds
- 11 Economic value: 50 seconds
- 12 The main objection: 60 seconds
- 13 Three more objections: 55 seconds
- 14 Verdict: 50 seconds
- 15 Close: 20 seconds

Total: 12 minutes.

If you have 10 minutes, cut the detail on card 07 and the second objection on card 13.
If you have 15 minutes, add one worked example on card 05 and one on card 11.

## How to use a card

Read the goal line before the slide appears.
Speak the say lines in your own words. Do not read them.
Touch the numbers on the point-at line.
End with the next line, then change the slide.
